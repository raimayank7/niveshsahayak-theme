We respect your privacy and handle personal data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, phone number, email address and message when you contact us through WhatsApp or email.

  • Your language choice: when you choose a language (for example हिन्दी or ਪੰਜਾਬੀ), it is saved in your own browser's storage so the website can show that language on your next visit. To load articles faster, the website may also keep a temporary list of our published articles in your browser for a few minutes. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your questions and service requests
  • To quote for, build, deliver and support the technology services you ask for
  • To contact you about investing, when you ask for a call back through our partner Wealthy's form (see "Investing through our partner platform" below)
  • To show the website in the language you chose
  • To improve the content and usability of this website
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations applicable to Mutual Fund Distributors

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.
